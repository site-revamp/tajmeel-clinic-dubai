# Tajmeel Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Tajmeel Clinic, part of Burjeel Holdings, is a premium healthcare and aesthetic medicine provider in the UAE, offering dentistry, dermatology, cosmetology, hair restoration and plastic surgery services.

The redesigned homepage targets patients seeking specialist-led dental and aesthetic treatments. It follows Tajmeel's **Beauty Redefined** positioning, with a focus on clear service discovery, premium presentation and a consistent patient enquiry experience.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Messaging and Value Proposition
- Strengthened the main headline to communicate Tajmeel's healthcare and aesthetic services.
- Added a concise introduction explaining the clinic's specialist offerings.
- Replaced generic calls to action with clearer treatment exploration and contact pathways.
- Addressed unfinished-looking elements, including visible zero counters and generic "Click Here" links.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage did not immediately communicate Tajmeel's value proposition, and some visible elements made the experience feel incomplete.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery and Information Hierarchy
- Organised treatments into five clear categories: Dentistry, Dermatology, Cosmetology, Hair Restoration and Plastic Surgery.
- Improved the presentation of specialties to make relevant treatments easier to identify.
- Established clearer pathways to explore individual services and specialty information.

**Problem addressed:** The previous specialties presentation lacked sufficient customer-oriented organisation, making treatment discovery more difficult.

### 3. More Consistent Contact and Conversion Journey
- Improved the visibility and consistency of contact information.
- Established clearer pathways for appointment enquiries and branch discovery.
- Addressed inconsistencies between the main Tajmeel website and the Dubai flagship microsite.
- Prioritised accessible contact actions for mobile visitors.

**Problem addressed:** Fragmented digital experiences and differing contact details could create confusion about the correct location and enquiry route.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more polished, premium healthcare and aesthetics-focused appearance.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and structured the homepage around clear specialty sections.
- **User Experience:** Simplified treatment discovery and strengthened navigation towards relevant services.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of contact information, branch discovery and conversion pathways.
- **Content Clarity:** Replaced generic messaging and calls to action with more descriptive, service-focused communication.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a more cohesive digital experience aligned with Tajmeel's premium brand positioning.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage aims to strengthen Tajmeel's premium positioning, make its multidisciplinary services easier to discover and provide a clearer, more consistent journey from treatment exploration to patient enquiry.
